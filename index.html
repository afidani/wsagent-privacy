WSAgent Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

WSAgent is a private AI-powered personal assistant operated by its owner through WhatsApp.

2. Information Processing

WSAgent processes WhatsApp messages, phone numbers, and information voluntarily provided by its authorized user to respond to requests and execute approved tasks.

3. Third-Party Services

WSAgent uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging and OpenAI's Codex for AI processing. Messages may be transmitted to these services as necessary to provide the assistant's functionality.

4. Data Storage

Conversation data and configuration may be stored locally on the operator's computer. Data is not sold to advertisers.

5. Data Access and Deletion

WSAgent is restricted to its authorized operator. The operator can request access to or deletion of locally stored personal data.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the WSAgent operator through the WhatsApp conversation used to access the service.
